      Understanding AI Image Metadata & C2PA Removal

      What is C2PA and Why Does Facebook Label My Images as "AI Info"?

      When generative AI models (such as ChatGPT DALL·E 3, Midjourney, or Adobe Firefly) create an image, they embed cryptographic provenance manifests defined by the Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA). These are stored inside specialized container chunks (c2pa, caBX, or JPEG APP11 JUMBF segments).

      When uploaded to Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram), automated ingest bots scan these container chunks and automatically apply an "AI info" badge. Standard EXIF cleaners that only look for camera tags fail to detect or strip these cryptographic chunks. TunerHimu AI Metadata Cleaner includes dedicated binary and canvas scrubbing engines to completely neutralize these markers.

      Traditional EXIF, GPS & Camera Metadata

      Digital smartphones and DSLR cameras append metadata including your exact GPS latitude/longitude, camera model, lens serial number, exposure settings, and timestamps in EXIF APP1 headers. Removing this data protects your geographic location and physical security before sharing images online.
